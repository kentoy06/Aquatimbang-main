Cover photo for the landing page
================================

Put a photo named hero.jpg in this folder (public/images/hero.jpg) and it
appears behind the headline on the landing page. Without it, the drawn
ocean scene is shown.

Tips: landscape, at least 1920 x 1080, under about 500 KB (compress it at
squoosh.app). The left side is darkened so the text stays readable, so a
photo with the main subject on the right works best. Use your own photo or
one you have permission to use.
